# Monthly Cash Flow Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Managed categories (rename, merge, archive, nesting), a monthly summary with a same-day comparison, a dashboard month card and month report, and search and filters on transactions. Backend, website and Android app.

**Architecture:**
- **Table:** `categories` (the ledger design's shape plus `name_key`, `icon`, `colour`). Transactions, templates and payee rules keep their `category` name; the table is the registry.
- **Logic:** `backend/app/api/categories.py` (seeding, resolving a name, rename, merge, archive, nest) and `backend/app/api/cash_flow.py` (pure month maths).
- **Routes:** `routes/categories.py` (`/categories`) and `routes/cash_flow.py` (`/cash-flow`). `GET /transactions` gains filters.
- **Migration:** `2026_11_13_0900-<rev>_add_categories.py`, after `e3b81f46a0d7`: the table, the `(user_id, date)` index, seeding.

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-cash-flow-design.md](../specs/2026-11-12-cash-flow-design.md).

## Global Constraints

- Money is `Decimal`, crosses the API as 2-decimal strings; sums in Python. Clients do no money arithmetic: percents and bar heights come from the server.
- Existing response shapes only gain keys. Clients that send a category name keep working.
- Every query is scoped to `user.id`. Validation errors are 422 via `field_error`; conflicts 409; other users' ids 404.
- The schema must match the models exactly (`alembic check` in `test_pg_migrations.py`).
- Web and Android each get their own UI. No chart library.

## Review Focus

1. Merging "Swiggy" into Food and dining moves transactions, templates and payee rules, and an old client sending "Swiggy" lands in Food and dining.
2. EMI principal, transfers (card payments) and trades never count as spending; EMI interest does.
3. 30 March compares with 1–28 February; a past month compares whole months.
4. An SMS whose rule names an archived category goes to review instead of failing.
