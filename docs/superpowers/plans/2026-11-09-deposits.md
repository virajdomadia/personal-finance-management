# PPF and Recurring Deposits Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Track a PPF account and recurring deposits as accounts: deposits as transfers from a bank, interest as income, computed maturity, interest estimates, RD instalment reminders, PPF interest reminders and Close RD, on the backend, website and Android app.

**Architecture:** Two new account types, `ppf` and `rd`, with four new columns on `accounts` (`opened_on`, `deposit_amount`, `tenure_months`, `deposits_at_opening`) and the existing `interest_rate`, `institution`, `due_day` and `pay_from_id`. All interest maths is pure functions in a new `backend/app/api/deposits.py`, fed from the account and its transactions. The accounts routes add a `deposit` figures object to every response. The transaction routes enforce what a deposit may carry, and a new `POST /accounts/{id}/close-rd` closes an RD in one request. Balances need no change: deposits move only through ordinary transactions.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, SQLAlchemy 2, Alembic, pytest (SQLite by default, PostgreSQL with `TEST_DATABASE_URL`). Next.js 16 (read `frontend/node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing pages). Expo SDK 58, Expo Router, react-native-paper (read the versioned Expo docs first, as `mobile/AGENTS.md` says). Client helpers are tested with `node --test` `.mjs` files.

**Spec:** [docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-deposits-design.md](../specs/2026-11-09-deposits-design.md). Its "Design calls made by Claude" list is accepted and binding.

## Global Constraints

- Money crosses the API as strings with 2 decimals (`fmt`). Rates as strings with 2 decimals. Dates as ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- All arithmetic is `Decimal` in Python, rounded `ROUND_HALF_UP` to paise **once, at the end** of each figure. Never floats, never SQL `SUM`. `Decimal ** Decimal` with a fractional exponent is fine in a local context with `prec = 34`.
- "Today" is `today_india()`. Day clamping uses `clamped_date` from `app/api/dates.py`.
- Validation errors: `RequestValidationError([field_error(field, message)])` → 422. Conflicts: `HTTPException(409, message)`. Missing or other-user ids → 404. Every query is scoped to `user.id`.
- No reminder ever posts on its own.
- Clients only display server figures. No interest arithmetic in TypeScript.
- The installed Android app must keep working against the new API. It groups accounts by its own `TYPE_ORDER`, so unknown types are simply not listed; keep it that way (no response shape the old app would choke on).
- Web and Android get their own UI. Never port one into the other.
- Subagents never start servers, emulators or browsers in the foreground. Long-running processes run in the background with a bounded readiness check (poll `http://localhost:8000/api/health` at most 30 times, 1 second apart), and are stopped at the end of the task.
- Browser checks use a throwaway test user on the local API, never the owner's data. **Never run `npm run build` in `frontend/`**: the owner's `next start` uses `frontend/.next`. Use `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npm run lint` instead.
- Commit after each task, ending messages with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. **An RD whose `opened_on` is the 31st.** Instalment days clamp per month (28/29 Feb), the maturity date clamps, and `whole_months(31 Jan, 29 Feb 2028)` is 1 (Task 1 tests).
2. **A PPF opened on 31 March vs 1 April.** 31 Mar 2020 is FY 2019-20 → matures 1 Apr 2035; 1 Apr 2020 → 1 Apr 2036 (Task 1 tests).
3. **Editing or deleting a recorded RD instalment.** The month becomes due again and `deposits_paid` drops by one; editing `deposits_paid` on the account never makes `deposits_at_opening` negative (Task 2 and Task 3 tests).
4. **Close RD for less than the balance (premature, with penalty), and Close RD when the bank is archived.** Penalty → "Fees and charges" expense and the RD ends at exactly 0.00; an archived bank → 422 on `account_id` and nothing written (Task 3 tests).
5. **An RD past its maturity date.** No months after the tenure are due, and `interest_so_far` stops growing at the maturity date (Task 1 tests).

### Task 1: Deposit maths

Pure functions, no database.

**Files:**
- Create: `backend/app/api/deposits.py`
- Test: `backend/tests/test_deposits_math.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Produces (all in `app/api/deposits.py`):
  - `class Dated(NamedTuple): date: date; amount: Decimal` — a signed movement on the deposit (+ in, − out).
  - `financial_year(day: date) -> int` — the FY's starting year (1 Apr 2026 → 2026, 31 Mar 2026 → 2025).
  - `fy_label(year: int) -> str` — `2026` → `"2026-27"`, `2099` → `"2099-00"`.
  - `add_months(day: date, months: int, anchor_day: int | None = None) -> date` — clamped to the month's length; `anchor_day` defaults to `day.day`.
  - `whole_months(start: date, end: date) -> int` — completed months; a month completes on the same day of the month, or on the month's last day when it is shorter. 0 when `end < start`.
  - `ppf_maturity(opened_on: date) -> date` — `date(financial_year(opened_on) + 16, 4, 1)`.
  - `rd_maturity(opened_on: date, tenure_months: int) -> date` — `add_months(opened_on, tenure_months)`.
  - `ppf_year_interest(opening: Decimal, moves: list[Dated], rate: Decimal, year: int, today: date) -> Decimal` — the spec's monthly lowest-balance rule over the months of FY `year` whose last day is ≤ `today`. Balance at end of day d = `opening` + moves dated ≤ d. Month's lowest = min of the balance at the end of the 5th and the balance after each move dated 6th…last day. Interest = Σ lowest × rate ÷ 1200, rounded once.
  - `ppf_interest_due(opening, moves, interest_dates: list[date], rate, added_on: date, today: date) -> tuple[int, Decimal] | None` — the year just ended (from its 31 March on), if it ended on or after `added_on` and no date in `interest_dates` is in [31 Mar of its end, 30 Mar a year later]. Returns `(year, ppf_year_interest(..., year, today))`.
  - `rd_maturity_value(amount: Decimal, rate: Decimal, tenure_months: int) -> Decimal`.
  - `rd_instalments(opened_on, deposit_amount, deposits_at_opening, transfers_in: list[Dated]) -> list[Dated]` — the opening instalments dated `add_months(opened_on, i)` for i < `deposits_at_opening`, then the recorded transfers in.
  - `rd_interest_so_far(instalments: list[Dated], rate, today, maturity: date, recorded_interest: Decimal) -> Decimal` — Σ amount × (q^(m/3) − 1) with m = `whole_months(date, min(today, maturity))`, minus `recorded_interest`, floored at 0.
  - `rd_due_months(opened_on, due_day, tenure_months, deposits_at_opening, transfer_dates: list[date], today) -> list[date]` — the instalment dates due, oldest first. Month i's date is `clamped_date` of month(`opened_on`) + i on `due_day`.
  - `rd_months_paid(transfer_dates: list[date]) -> int` — distinct calendar months.
